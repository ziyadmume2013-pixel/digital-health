Ziyad Abdela Mume, MPH, is a senior Public Health and digital Health Information Systems (HIS) advisor with over 10 years of experience leading Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability, and Learning (MEAL) systems across Ethiopia. Specialized in bridging clinical workflows with modern digital tools, Ziyad has spearheaded regional surveillance databases, DHIS2 vaccine-safety integrations, and eCHIS community health configurations in collaboration with UNICEF, WHO, and the Ministry of Health. He has managed comprehensive results frameworks and indicator tracking for major donor-funded programs (USAID/PEPFAR, FCDO, Plan International, and Save the Children), with proven success in elevating partner data quality scores from 63% to 87%. An expert in KoboToolbox, CommCare, ODK, REDCap, and Power BI dashboards, Ziyad holds an MPH from Haramaya University and is certified in MEAL DPro, Clinical Research Data Management, and Global Health Informatics.
